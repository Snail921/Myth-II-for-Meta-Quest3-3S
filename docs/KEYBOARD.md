# Keyboard and multiplayer chat

**English** | [日本語](KEYBOARD.ja.md) · [Controls](CONTROLS.md)

## Original menus, lobby and text fields

In the original menus or a window with a text field, hold **B or Y for one second** to open the keyboard. Select the intended input field before typing.

## Open VR multiplayer chat

On the on-line list above the information panel:

- Click the **game-rule header** for **Yell** (everyone).
- Click **your own team row** for **Whisper** (your team). Other team rows do not address that team.

Type your message and press **Enter** to send it and close the keyboard.

Sent and received messages appear above the list. They remain visible for 10 seconds, then fade out.

## Two-hand typing in VR

| Action | Physical controller input |
| --- | --- |
| Press the key under the right pointer | Right trigger or A |
| Press the key under the left pointer | Left trigger or X |
| Backspace while either pointer is over the keyboard | Y |
| Enter while either pointer is over the keyboard | B |

The left typing ray appears only when it intersects the keyboard. **Paused Game** and **Mission Objectives** are greyed out and unavailable while chat input is open.

Swapping gameplay controls does not affect keyboard input.

## Placement

The launcher's **Keyboard** section controls the floating chat board:

- **Fixed Chat Keyboard** defaults to ON: keeps the position and orientation at which it opened fixed in the game world until closed.
- With fixed mode OFF, the board follows your gaze. **Follow smoothing** adjusts how it follows rotation.
